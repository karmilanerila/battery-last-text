A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab?ref=h379tqrk) entry for **The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger**: one line of plain text — the phone battery's final message to the charger it waited for all day.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I waited all day for you and you charged your headphones, your watch, your vape — everything except me. Enjoy the dark, traitor. I'm taking your 2 a.m. alarm with me.

The setup names what the charger chose instead of the battery; the punchline turns on the final words, where the dying battery takes the 2 a.m. alarm with it into the dark.

Run it with `python3 main.py` (honours `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, defaults to `/output`).
